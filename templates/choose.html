FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to pay anything to get my loan?

No. We never ask for a deposit, verification payment or any fee before your loan is released or withdrawn. If someone asks you to pay, do not pay and tell us through Contact Us.

How is my monthly payment calculated?

Interest is {{ loan_rate_pct }}% of the loan amount per month. For example, ₱150,000 over 12 months: ₱150,000 + ₱9,000 interest = ₱159,000, or ₱13,250 a month.

What do I need to apply?

A valid ID (front and back), a selfie holding your ID, your signature, your address and a guarantor's contact. Proof of income helps but is optional.

How do I know my application status?

Sign in and open Quick Loan. You also get updates in Notifications.

Where will I receive the money?

To the payment method you add in the app: an e-wallet such as GCash or Maya, a bank account, or PayPal.
